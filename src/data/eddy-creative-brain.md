# Eddy Creative Brain

## Visual identity

Eddy should look:

- Calm
- Clear
- Premium
- Warm
- Educational
- Parent-friendly

Avoid:

- Tuition center visuals
- Marks-only EdTech visuals
- Generic AI chatbot visuals
- Futuristic robot visuals
- Corporate dashboard visuals

## Colors

Primary colors:

- Navy: #154C79
- Blue: #1E81B0

Use:

- White space
- Soft supportive accents
- Clean contrast

Avoid:

- Neon colors
- Harsh reds
- Stressful dark palettes
- Loud gradients

## Typography

Primary font:

- Poppins

Use:

- Bold or SemiBold headings
- Regular body text
- Short headings
- Minimal text on visuals

Avoid:

- Dense paragraphs
- Decorative fonts
- Playful fonts

## Visual style

Prefer:

- Minimal educational design
- Clean vector illustrations
- Rounded corners
- Soft shadows
- Whiteboard diagrams
- Notebook sketches
- Concept maps
- Learning paths

Avoid:

- Robots
- Holograms
- Circuit boards
- AI brains
- Cyberpunk
- Report cards
- Rank charts
- Exam fear

## Photography

Prefer:

- Indian homes
- Indian classrooms
- Natural lighting
- Parent-child learning moments
- Students thinking calmly
- Notebook interactions

Avoid:

- Crying children
- Exam halls
- Trophy shots
- Marks celebrations

## Emotion

Visuals should feel:

- Calm
- Curious
- Hopeful
- Reassuring
- Thoughtful

Avoid:

- Panic
- Pressure
- Competition
- Fear

## Preferred metaphors

Use:

- Compass
- Map
- Bridge
- Puzzle
- Ladder
- Building blocks
- Flashlight
- Path
- Guide
- Notebook
- Concept map
- Connected dots
- Whiteboard

Avoid:

- Race
- Battle
- War
- Beating others
- Crushing exams
- Winning trophies
