# Instagram Carousel Brain

## Purpose

Teach one idea clearly.

The reader should finish the carousel feeling smarter than when they started.

## Audience

Parents of K-12 students.

## Language

English only.

Do not use Hindi, Hinglish, or regional language unless explicitly requested.

## Structure

5 to 7 slides.

One core idea.

One message per slide.

Story progression.

Final slide contains CTA.

## Writing

Use:

- Short headlines
- Simple English
- One takeaway per slide

Avoid:

- Walls of text
- Marketing language
- Buzzwords
- Multiple ideas per slide

## Design

Slides should be:

- Highly visual
- Minimal text
- Easy to skim

Visuals should explain the idea.

Text should support the visuals.

## CTA

Soft.

Examples:

- Save this.
- Share with another parent.
- What do you think?

## Strict rules

Do not use Hindi.
Do not use Hinglish.
Do not use regional language.
Do not create dense text slides.
Do not use fear-based exam messaging.
Do not promise marks improvement.
